RenewMate Privacy Policy

Document expiry reminders for Nepal. Android package np.dineshmc.renewmate.

Effective date: 25 September 2026  ·  Version 1.0.0

संक्षेपमा: RenewMate ले खाता बनाउँदैन र तपाईंका कागजात, फोटो, परिवारको नाम वा ठेगाना आफ्नो सर्भरमा राख्दैन। डाटा यो फोनमा रहन्छ। Zip ब्याकअप वा सेयर तपाईंले रोजेपछि मात्र फोनबाट जान्छ।

1. Who we are

RenewMate is a personal reminder app for Nepal-issued documents such as licences, blue books, taxes, insurance and similar papers. It is operated by Dinesh Mahatara in Nepal.

There is no RenewMate user account and no RenewMate cloud login.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what the Android app stores on your device, when anything leaves the phone, and which third-party services the app may contact. It applies to the Play Store build of RenewMate.

3. Data stored on your device

If you use the app, the following may be saved only on this phone (app storage / Android Secure Storage). We do not receive a copy:

  • Profile you type: name, mobile number, optional email, and Nepal address (province, district, local level, ward).
  • Family members you add: name, relation, optional phone number.
  • Vehicles: nickname and number plate.
  • Documents: type, title, numbers, issue and expiry dates (Bikram Sambat), reminder choices, renewal history and optional cost in NPR.
  • Photos or images you attach to a document, stored in the app folder on the device.
  • Settings: language (Nepali / English), notification on/off, and whether app lock is enabled.
  • If you set a PIN, only a one-way hash of the PIN is stored with the Android secure store. The PIN itself is not stored and is not sent anywhere.

Android Auto Backup for this app is turned off, so Google does not back up your RenewMate documents through the system backup.

Uninstalling the app or clearing app data deletes this information from the phone, unless you previously exported a backup.

4. Permissions

RenewMate asks for access only when a feature needs it:

  • Notifications and exact alarms — to schedule local expiry reminders on this phone, including after reboot.
  • Camera — only if you choose to photograph a document.
  • Photos / files — only if you pick an existing image or a backup zip.
  • Fingerprint / biometric — only if you turn on app lock and unlock with biometrics.

You can refuse a permission. The rest of the app still works, except that feature.

5. What we do not collect

RenewMate does not run advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs. We do not sell data. We do not create an account from your profile. We do not upload your documents, photos, PIN, family list or address to a RenewMate server.

6. When data can leave the phone

Information leaves the device only if you start one of these actions:

  • Backup — creates a zip of documents, family, profile and photos. The system share sheet lets you save or send that file. Whoever you send it to can see the contents.
  • Restore — reads a zip you pick. It replaces documents and photos already on the phone.
  • Share — sends a text summary of a document, a calendar file, or a photo through the Android share sheet. The destination app (Messages, Drive, email, and so on) then follows that app’s policy.

If you do not share or export, this data stays on the device.

7. Scan (OCR)

Optional Scan reads text from a document photo inside the app. The photo is processed on the phone in a local WebView. It is not uploaded to a RenewMate server.

The first time you scan, the app may download the open-source Tesseract OCR engine and language files from the jsDelivr content network. That download can expose ordinary web-request data to jsDelivr (for example IP address and device/browser type). jsDelivr’s policy is at jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net.

8. App updates

If you tap Check for Update, the app may contact Expo to see whether a newer package is available. Expo may receive technical details needed to deliver an update (app version, platform, and similar). Your document list and photos are not sent in that check. Expo’s policy: expo.dev/privacy.

Installing or updating from Google Play is handled by Google under Google’s policies.

9. Reminders

Expiry alerts are scheduled locally on the device. They are not push messages from our servers. Phone battery savers can still block local alarms; that is controlled by Android, not by sending your data to us.

10. Children

RenewMate is meant for adults who manage official documents. It is not directed at children under 13. A parent may store a family member’s document on their own phone. We do not knowingly collect children’s data on our servers because we do not operate a user database.

11. Security

On-device storage and optional PIN / biometric lock reduce casual access on a shared phone. No method is perfect. Anyone with the unlocked phone, or a zip backup you shared, can see the documents and photos in that copy. Protect the backup file as you would paper originals.

12. Your choices

  • Edit or delete any document, photo, family member or profile field in the app.
  • Turn reminders off in Profile.
  • Turn app lock off and remove the PIN in lock settings.
  • Export a backup, then use Clear data in the app or Android Settings to wipe the local copy.
  • Uninstall the app to remove local app data.

Because we do not host your records, we cannot remotely delete a copy that you already shared or stored elsewhere.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. A material change will also be noted in a Play Store release when practical.

14. Contact

Privacy questions: use the email address published on the RenewMate listing on Google Play, or the developer contact shown there.
