This Privacy Policy explains how शिक्षा कानून संग्रह (Edu Kanun) handles information when you use the app.

ⓘ Non-official status

This app is not an official application of the Government of Nepal or any ministry. It does not represent a government entity and does not provide government services.

Edu Kanun is an independent, unofficial reference collection. For authoritative information, consult the original government sources listed below.

01
Ministry of Education, Science and Technology https://moest.gov.np/
02
Nepal Law Commission https://lawcommission.gov.np/
03
Nepal Gazette https://rajpatra.dop.gov.np/
04
Center for Education and Human Resource Development https://cehrd.gov.np/

01 Information we collect

The app does not require an account, password, location, contacts, camera, or payment details.

Bookmarks, downloaded documents, theme preferences, and reading progress are stored only on your device. This information is not sent to our servers.

✓

No account required. You can use the app without registering or creating a password.

✓

Local storage. Your bookmarks, preferences, downloads, and reading progress remain on your device.

✓

No app analytics or ads. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

02 Documents and internet use

The document list is loaded from a public Google Drive folder. PDF files are downloaded to your device only when you open or save them.

Opening official source links uses your device's browser. The websites and services you visit may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

03 Sharing, ratings, and other apps

Features such as Rate, Update, Share, and “More apps” may open Google Play or your device's share sheet.

These services are operated by third parties and are subject to their own privacy policies. Edu Kanun does not control the privacy practices of those external services.

04 Feedback

If you choose to send feedback, the app opens your device's email application and prepares a message to dineshmahatarachhetri@gmail.com .

The message includes the name, email address, and feedback message you entered. The information is sent only if you choose to send the email.

05 Children

Edu Kanun is a general reference tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
