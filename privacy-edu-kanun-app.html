Information we collect

The app does not require an account, password, or payment details. Bookmarks, downloads, theme preference, and reading progress are stored only on your device. This data is not sent to our servers.

Documents and internet use

The document list is loaded from a public Google Drive folder. PDF files are downloaded to your device only when you open or save them. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Sharing, ratings, and other apps

Rate, update, share, and “More apps” actions may open Google Play or your device share sheet. Those services have their own privacy policies.
