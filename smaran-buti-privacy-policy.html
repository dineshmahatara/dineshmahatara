Privacy Policy — Smaran Buti

Last updated: August 2026

Smaran Buti (“the App”) is a memory-training app. We respect your privacy.

1. What we collect

  • Name and email if you register or use Google Sign-In
  • A device identifier for guest/device accounts and security
  • Learning progress, notes, memory palaces, settings, practice scores
  • Google Play purchase tokens for Premium subscriptions

Some data stays on your device. If you create an account it may also be stored on our servers.

2. Ads

The free version may show Google AdMob ads. AdMob may use advertising ID and coarse location. We do not sell personal profiles. Premium can remove ads.

3. Payments

Subscriptions use Google Play Billing. We do not store card numbers.

4. Notifications

Daily reminders need notification permission, which you can decline.

5. Account deletion

In the App: Settings → Delete account. You can also request deletion at Account deletion.

6. Children

The App is intended for ages 13+. Younger learners should use it with a parent or guardian.
